HOW TO PLAY

Rules, objectives, and grid variations

Basic Rules

Tic Tac Toe is a two-player game where players alternate taking turns placing their mark on an empty grid square.

  1. Player 1 plays as X and Player 2 (or AI) plays as O.
  2. Players take turns clicking an open square to place their symbol.
  3. The first player to align their symbols in an unbroken row, column, or diagonal wins the game.
  4. If all spaces are filled and neither player has achieved a line, the match ends in a draw.

Dynamic Matrix Sizing

Before starting a game in the Arena, you can select the dimensions of your board:

  • 3 x 3: Standard rules — 3 marks in a row to win.
  • 6 x 6: Expanded arena — connect 6 in a row to win.
  • 9 x 9: Large grid challenge — connect 9 in a row to win.
