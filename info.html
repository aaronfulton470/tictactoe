HOW TO PLAY

Master the rules, mechanics, and winning strategies

Standard Rules

  1. Turn Order: Player X always opens the match, followed alternately by Player O.
  2. Valid Moves: Click or tap any unoccupied square on the active grid. Once placed, a mark cannot be moved or replaced.
  3. Winning the Match: Complete an unbroken horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line of 3 matching symbols.
  4. The Cat's Game (Draw): If all 9 squares are occupied without a winning triplet, the match ends in a stalemate.
